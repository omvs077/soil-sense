# Agronomic Reference Standards & Soil Fertility Guidelines

This document details the agricultural scientific benchmarks utilized in Soil-Sense, derived from **ICAR (Indian Council of Agricultural Research)** and **FAO (Food and Agriculture Organization)** soil health card standards.

---

## 1. Primary Macronutrients

### Nitrogen ($N$)
- **Unit**: mg/kg (or ppm; $1\text{ mg/kg} \approx 2.24\text{ kg/ha}$)
- **Low**: $< 140\text{ mg/kg}$ ($< 280\text{ kg/ha}$)
- **Medium / Optimal**: $140 - 280\text{ mg/kg}$ ($280 - 560\text{ kg/ha}$)
- **High**: $> 280\text{ mg/kg}$ ($> 560\text{ kg/ha}$)
- **Role**: Essential for vegetative leaf growth and chlorophyll formation.

### Phosphorus ($P$)
- **Unit**: mg/kg (available $P_2O_5$)
- **Low**: $< 10\text{ mg/kg}$
- **Medium / Optimal**: $10 - 25\text{ mg/kg}$
- **High**: $> 25\text{ mg/kg}$
- **Role**: Promotes early root formation, seed development, and cellular energy transfer (ATP).

### Potassium ($K$)
- **Unit**: mg/kg (available $K_2O$)
- **Low**: $< 110\text{ mg/kg}$
- **Medium / Optimal**: $110 - 280\text{ mg/kg}$
- **High**: $> 280\text{ mg/kg}$
- **Role**: Regulates stomatal opening, water retention, and disease/drought resistance.

## 2. Soil Reaction & Physicochemical Properties

### Soil pH
- **Strongly Acidic**: $< 5.5$ (affects nutrient uptake, aluminum toxicity risk)
- **Moderately Acidic**: $5.5 - 6.5$
- **Ideal / Neutral**: $6.5 - 7.5$ (peak availability for most crops)
- **Moderately Alkaline**: $7.5 - 8.5$
- **Strongly Alkaline / Sodic**: $> 8.5$ (phosphorus and micronutrient lockup)

### Electrical Conductivity ($EC$)
- **Unit**: dS/m ($1\text{ dS/m} = 1\text{ mS/cm} = 1\text{ mmho/cm}$)
- **Normal / Non-saline**: $< 1.0\text{ dS/m}$
- **Slightly Saline**: $1.0 - 2.0\text{ dS/m}$
- **Moderately Saline**: $2.0 - 4.0\text{ dS/m}$ (yields of sensitive crops restricted)
- **Severely Saline**: $> 4.0\text{ dS/m}$ (severe osmotic stress)

### Soil Moisture
- **Critical Wilting Point**: $< 15\%$
- **Adequate / Available Water**: $20 - 65\%$ (field capacity depending on soil texture)
- **Waterlogged / Saturated**: $> 80\%$ (poor aeration and root hypoxia)

---

## 3. Fertility Class Definitions

| Fertility Class | Typical Soil Conditions | Management Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **High** | Balanced NPK within optimal bands, neutral pH ($6.2 - 7.5$), low EC ($< 1.0$), adequate moisture. | Maintenance fertilization, cover cropping, organic matter preservation. |
| **Medium** | Minor deficiency in one macronutrient, slight pH deviation ($5.8 - 6.4$ or $7.6 - 8.2$), moderate moisture. | Targeted soil amendments (e.g. compost, specific starter fertilizer). |
| **Low** | Severe depletion in multiple macronutrients (N, P, or K), extreme acidity ($< 5.5$) or alkalinity ($> 8.5$), or excessive salinity ($EC > 3.0$). | Major soil reclamation: liming for acid soils, gypsum for sodic soils, substantial organic composting. |
